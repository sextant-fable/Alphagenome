# Literature and Style Protocol

## Question

How do Nature Portfolio sequence-to-function method papers turn a computational
advance into a coherent method Article rather than a collection of performance
plots?

## Source Standard

- Use Nature Portfolio article pages and publisher pages as primary sources for
  paper metadata, figure sequence and submission requirements.
- Use CrossRef or PubMed only to verify metadata when a publisher page is
  incomplete.
- Cite the original paper for its own method, data and results. Do not repeat
  text or recreate its figures.
- Record only structural lessons: claim order, evidence type, figure role and
  presentation strategy.

## Search Families

1. DNA sequence-to-function, gene-expression and RNA-seq coverage prediction.
2. Variant-effect prediction and external QTL/perturbation validation.
3. Foundation-model transfer, fairness of public-model comparison and component
   ablation.
4. *C. elegans* methods papers that make a model organism the experimental
   advantage rather than a narrowness excuse.

## Selection Rule

The audit prioritizes one direct RNA-seq model, two Nature Methods sequence
models, one Nature unified-model paper, one fair foundation-model benchmark,
one interpretation/perturbation paper and one *C. elegans* methods paper.
The set is illustrative for structure, not a claim of exhaustive prior art.

## Output Rule

Every planned figure must map to a project-specific claim and data source. It
may borrow a published paper's evidence role, but never its prose, visual asset
or unsupported conclusion.
