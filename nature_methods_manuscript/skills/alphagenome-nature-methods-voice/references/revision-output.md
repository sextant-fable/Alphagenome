# Revision Output Patterns

## Results Paragraph

Return one claim-first paragraph with:

1. The question or comparison.
2. The measured effect, analytic unit and uncertainty.
3. The limited interpretation tied to that evidence.

Keep full statistical details in the legend or Methods when they would obscure
the sentence, but do not omit the comparison direction or unit.

## Figure Legend

Use `Fig. N | Declarative title.` Then explain panels in order, define symbols,
state n and the statistical interval, and point to Source Data. Do not repeat
the whole Results paragraph in the legend.

## Methods Paragraph

State inputs, operation, output, fixed parameters and validation hook. Prefer
precise verbs such as `grouped`, `aligned`, `aggregated`, `evaluated` and
`bootstrapped` over generic verbs such as `leveraged` or `utilized`.

## Boundary Sentence

Name the exact condition where the result stops. Example shape:

`This result establishes [capability] for [frozen data regime], but does not
evaluate [different regime].`

Do not add extra rhetorical apology before or after the boundary.
