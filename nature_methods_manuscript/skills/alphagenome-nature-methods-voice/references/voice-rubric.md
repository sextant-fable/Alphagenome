# Natural-Prose Rubric

## Aim

Make the manuscript sound like a precise account by authors who know the data,
not like a generic model-generated celebration of a method. The goal is not to
make every sentence sound different. The goal is to remove writing that hides
the scientific message.

## High-Value Edits

| Pattern | Preferred repair |
| --- | --- |
| Empty importance claim | Name the task, comparison or biological consequence. |
| Generic novelty adjective | State the actual change in data, model or evaluation. |
| Mechanical sequence words | Show the causal or evidential relation directly. |
| Long noun stack | Put the experimental object first and split only when needed. |
| Vague subject such as `this` or `it` | Name the model, dataset, score or analysis. |
| Repeated `We show that` | Vary the sentence around the measured observation or comparison. |
| Decorative contrast | Keep only contrasts that change the interpretation. |
| Unexplained `robust` or `generalizable` | State the test strata, source isolation and effect. |

## Review Flags, Not Automatic Replacements

Review the local context whenever it includes:

- `leverage`, `delve`, `underscore`, `unveil`, `pivotal`, `transformative`,
  `groundbreaking`, `revolutionary`, `comprehensive`, `powerful`, `robust`,
  `novel`, `notably`, `it is worth noting`, `first and foremost`;
- a broad `This demonstrates` sentence with no nearby result or figure;
- three parallel claims with no evidence distinction;
- a dash used instead of a logical relation;
- an unsupported `first`, `state-of-the-art` or universal assertion.

The presence of a flag does not prove bad writing. Technical words such as
`robust` may be appropriate if the evidence establishes the stated robustness.
Keep valid sentences rather than changing them for cosmetic variety.

## Manuscript-Body Form

Use connected paragraphs in the abstract, Introduction, Results and Discussion.
Use lists where reproducibility benefits: Online Methods, checklists,
supplementary materials, data availability and figure legends.
