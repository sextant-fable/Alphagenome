# Writing Sequence and Quality Gates

## Order of Work After Authorization to Draft

1. Freeze external placeholders and fill the terminology ledger.
2. Assemble figure/source-data contracts and finalize the six-item display
   budget.
3. Draft Online Methods from the frozen manifests.
4. Draft Results one subsection at a time against the figure contract.
5. Draft the Introduction after Results have stable evidence language.
6. Draft Discussion from the supported claims and explicit boundaries.
7. Draft title and abstract last.
8. Write legends, availability statements and cover letter after all main
   wording is stable.

## Per-Section Gate

A section is ready for the next stage only if all checks pass:

- Every paragraph has one job: context, gap, approach, result, comparison,
  interpretation, implication or boundary.
- Every claim maps to a figure/table/method or verified citation.
- Every number has a unit, analytic population, statistic and source-data row.
- Canonical terms match `TERMINOLOGY_LEDGER.md`.
- Placeholder tokens are either filled from a frozen artifact or remain visibly
  unresolved; no generic replacement sentence is allowed.
- The local voice skill reports no critical claim-boundary, terminology or
  mechanical-prose finding.

## Manuscript-Level Gates

| Gate | Owner evidence | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scientific freeze | External protocol, artifacts and reviewer check. | Any unfilled primary external field. |
| Statistical freeze | Primary units, CIs, multiplicity and source data audited. | A figure uses a different n than its legend. |
| Visual freeze | Final-size QA, vector text, source data and color audit. | A panel has no distinct claim or ambiguous labels. |
| Reuse freeze | Code/data/checkpoint/environment release tested cleanly. | A reader cannot reproduce the published example. |
| Submission freeze | Article limits, Reporting Summary, availability, AI disclosure and cover letter rechecked against current official guidance. | Current journal instructions conflict with local plan. |

## Tone Gate

The manuscript should sound like a technically exact account by authors who
know what their evidence establishes. Remove inflated significance statements,
formulaic scene-setting, fake rhetorical contrast and defensive disclaimers.
Keep the actual boundary in the relevant sentence or paragraph; do not hide it
in a generic limitation list.
