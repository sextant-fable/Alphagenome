# Part 1: Title, Abstract and Front Matter Plan

## Role

Give a broad methods reader the problem, technical move, decisive external
evidence and practical biological consequence in less than 150 words, without
turning the abstract into a software inventory.

## Title Plan

Select the final title only after the external endpoint is frozen. Candidate
shapes, not final titles:

1. `Species-adapted sequence-to-track modeling predicts C. elegans RNA programs`
2. `A species-adapted sequence model links C. elegans genomes to RNA regulation`
3. `Sequence-to-RNA-track adaptation enables regulatory variant scoring in C. elegans`

Use candidate 1 if the main contribution is the prediction benchmark. Use
candidate 2 only if the external biological application is decisive. Use
candidate 3 only if the independent variant endpoint passes its prespecified
primary criterion. Do not insert `novel`, `powerful`, `foundation`, `first` or
an unsupported performance adjective.

## Abstract Blueprint: Six Sentences, 135-150 Words

| Sentence | Job | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Define the biological prediction problem and why worm RNA resources matter. | Verified field framing and citations. |
| 2 | Name the gap: lack of a fair species-adapted sequence-to-RNA evaluation. | Verified prior-art synthesis. |
| 3 | Introduce the full model and its input/output scope. | Frozen model specification. |
| 4 | State the external benchmark comparison with the decisive effect and interval. | `EXTERNAL_MODEL_B_EFFECT_AND_CI`. |
| 5 | State the independent cis-eQTL outcome, with its endpoint. | `EXTERNAL_VARIANT_OR_PERTURBATION_RESULT`. |
| 6 | State the released capability and bounded biological implication. | Release manifest and demonstrated scope. |

The abstract must not lead with P phase names, detailed training steps, the
internal locked Pearson values or the DPY-27 diagnostic. It must not claim
cross-species transfer if the external benchmark is same-species.

## Front Matter Checklist

- Article title and running title.
- Full author list, affiliations, corresponding authors and ORCIDs.
- Author-approved method name, if any.
- 3-6 author keywords built from search terms rather than marketing language.
- Contributions, funding, competing interests and acknowledgements.
- AI-use disclosure prepared from an auditable log of assistive use and human
  verification; no LLM appears as an author.
- Data Availability, Code Availability, Reporting Summary and Software
  Submission Checklist cross-referenced to Part 7.

## Do Not Draft Yet

This plan intentionally contains no title or abstract prose. The first draft
must be created only after the external placeholders are populated and the
terminology ledger is locked.
