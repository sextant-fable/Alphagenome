# Part 2: Introduction Plan

## Target

Four paragraphs, approximately 430-500 words, no heading in the submitted
Article. The reader should reach the final paragraph knowing exactly why a
species-adapted RNA coverage model needs both fair external validation and an
independent biological use case.

## Paragraph Map

| Paragraph | Single job | Content to include | Evidence/citation class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Context | DNA sequence-to-function models convert long genomic sequence into functional measurements and make regulatory hypotheses testable. | Primary sequence-model papers; no generic history. |
| 2 | Bottleneck | RNA-seq coverage integrates transcriptional and transcript-structure signals, while C. elegans has a rich but heterogeneous public resource and a distinct species-transfer problem. | Borzoi-type task framing plus worm RNA resource provenance. |
| 3 | Gap | Existing models establish long-context or multimodal prediction in other systems, but do not establish a harmonized, study-isolated species-adaptation benchmark with a C. elegans regulatory-use endpoint. | Fair synthesis of Enformer, Borzoi, AlphaGenome, relevant worm work. |
| 4 | Present study | State the model, data resource, external benchmark, component attribution and independent variant application in that order. | Only frozen project evidence. |

## Required Transitions

- Paragraph 1 to 2: move from general potential to the specific RNA coverage
  object, not from importance to an architecture list.
- Paragraph 2 to 3: explain why a public compendium alone does not establish
  cross-study utility.
- Paragraph 3 to 4: state the contribution as a direct response to the
  evaluation gap, then preview the evidence ladder without numbers.

## Terminology to Define at First Use

- `sequence-to-RNA-track model`
- `RNA-seq coverage`
- `study-isolated benchmark`
- `unseen biological condition`
- Final author-approved method name, followed by its short name only after the
  definition.

## Exclusions

- No detailed P6/P9/P10 phase history.
- No claims about eQTL performance before Fig. 4 evidence.
- No full baseline catalogue; defer exact architectures and eligibility to
  Online Methods and Table 1.
- No sentence that makes C. elegans sound merely like a small or convenient
  genome. Frame it as a genetically tractable organism with a defined data
  resource and regulatory problem.
