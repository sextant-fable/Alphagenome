## Non-Negotiable Narrative Order

1. Establish the data asset, predictive task and technical intervention.
2. Define what external means in this paper before any accuracy number appears.
3. Lead with the fair external comparison, not internal model-selection data.
4. Explain distinct P9 component effects with the registered factorial design.
5. End Results with an external biological use case that was frozen before
   score inspection.
6. State operational reuse and boundaries without turning audit controls into
   headline biology.

## What the Paper Will Not Claim

- Cross-species generalization unless a separate cross-species frozen benchmark
  is actually completed.
- Causal rescue, absolute expression recovery or DPY-27 dosage-compensation
  recovery from the P10 diagnostic.
- Universal superiority over methods that cannot be made technically comparable.
- A necessary contribution from the learned 1-bp head on the present primary
  endpoint.
- Independent reproduction before a clean external reproduction exists.

## Method Name Rule

No branded method name is currently author-approved. Use `the full model` or
`Model B` in planning documents. Select a final method name before the first
draft and add it to the terminology ledger; do not invent one during prose
