# Nature Methods Manuscript Workspace

## Status

This workspace is a planning, governance and owner-authorized initial-draft
package for a prospective Nature Methods Article on AlphaGenome-style
sequence-to-RNA-track modeling in *Caenorhabditis elegans*. The working draft
is a coherent assumed-results scaffold; it is not a submission record while
the external tokens remain unresolved.

The narrative is planned on the user's requested assumption that the external
benchmark and independent biological application have been completed under a
frozen protocol. Their dataset identities, metrics, confidence intervals and
figures are represented only by protected placeholders until their signed
artifacts are available. This prevents a smooth narrative from becoming a
fictional one.

## Article Target

- Journal: *Nature Methods*
- Article type: Article
- Intended main-text architecture: unheaded Introduction, Results, Discussion,
  Online Methods
- Budget to plan against: 150-word abstract, 3,000-word main text target and no
  more than six main display items. The current official guidance remains the
  controlling source if it changes before submission.

## Initial Draft Gate

Before replacing an external token or labeling the manuscript final, freeze the
following inputs in `plans/08_external_benchmark_placeholder_contract.md`:

1. Exact external benchmark datasets, laboratory or study isolation rule, and
   unseen biological condition.
2. Harmonized baseline protocol, permitted tuning budget and all final metrics.
3. External biological-validation endpoint and its prespecified statistical
   analysis.
4. Code, checkpoint, data and software-release locations.
5. Author-approved method name, author list and affiliations.

The completed internal P9 component-attribution result is usable evidence. The
P10 DPY-27 result is a negative internal diagnostic and must not be recast as a
successful biological validation.
